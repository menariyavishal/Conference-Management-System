## Navigation Bar

### Overview
A professional, clean navigation bar that appears at the top of every page with a fixed or sticky position.

### Components
- **Logo/Brand Name**: "ConferenceHub" or company logo on the left
- **Navigation Links** (aligned right):
  - Home
  - Features
  - How It Works
  - Pricing
  - About Us
  - Contact
  - Blog/Resources
  
- **User Authentication Buttons** (far right):
  - Login Button (Outlined/Ghost style)
  - Sign Up Button (Solid primary color)

### Design Details
- Background: White or light gradient
- Logo height: ~40px
- Navigation links: Medium font weight, dark gray text
- Active link: Underline or highlight in primary color
- Mobile: Hamburger menu icon

## FAQ Section

### Section Title
"Frequently Asked Questions"

### FAQ Items (Accordion format)

**Q1: What is the difference between the Basic and Professional plans?**
- A: The Basic plan is ideal for smaller events with up to 500 attendees and basic management features. The Professional plan includes advanced features like peer review system, payment processing, and detailed analytics, supporting up to 2,500 attendees.

**Q2: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?**
- A: Yes, you can change your plan at any time. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle. If upgrading mid-cycle, we'll prorate the difference.

**Q3: Is there a free trial available?**
- A: Absolutely! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start your trial. You'll have full access to all features of your chosen plan.

**Q4: What payment methods do you accept?**
- A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), bank transfers, and PayPal for monthly and annual payments.

**Q5: Is my data secure?**
- A: Yes, we implement industry-leading security standards including SSL encryption, regular security audits, and GDPR compliance. Your data is backed up daily and stored in secure data centers.

**Q6: Do you offer onboarding support?**
- A: Yes, all plans include access to our comprehensive knowledge base. Professional and Enterprise plans include dedicated support and onboarding assistance.

**Q7: Can I customize the attendee portal with my branding?**
- A: Professional and Enterprise plans include full portal customization with your logo, colors, and custom domain options.

**Q8: What happens to my data if I cancel?**
- A: You can export all your data anytime. We retain your data for 30 days after cancellation, after which it's permanently deleted.

## Design Principles

1. **Professional & Clean**: Minimal clutter, professional color scheme
2. **Accessible**: High contrast ratios, proper heading hierarchy, keyboard navigation
3. **Responsive**: Mobile-first design, works seamlessly on all screen sizes
4. **Fast**: Optimized images, efficient code, quick load times
5. **User-Centric**: Clear CTAs, intuitive navigation, easy information discovery
6. **Trust-Building**: Testimonials, security badges, clear pricing, no hidden fees

- **Headlines**: Modern sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat, Inter, Poppins)
- **Body Text**: Readable sans-serif (e.g., Open Sans, Roboto, Lato)
